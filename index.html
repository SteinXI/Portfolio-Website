ABOUT ME

Learning Through Experience

Every experience offers an opportunity to learn, reflect and develop a broader perspective.

My professional journey has taken me through different areas of management, from operational auditing and human resources to academic programme coordination, education counselling and international partnerships. Working across these functions has given me opportunities to understand organisations from different perspectives and appreciate the relationship between planning, implementation and day-to-day operations.

I approach responsibilities by seeking to understand the situation, examining available information and considering possible risks and outcomes. I value accuracy, reliability and thoughtful problem-solving, while recognising the importance of working with people from different backgrounds and adapting to unfamiliar situations.

Alongside my professional experience, further study in business and management, including my MBA, has encouraged me to reflect on practical challenges from broader perspectives. I view learning as an ongoing process, shaped by experience, reflection and a willingness to develop beyond familiar areas.
